## Checklist: ground layout

- Paths, pavements and gardens follow the lot's new outline: no odd arcs, kinks or loops, no pieces left at the
  old place.
- Ground items stay flush with the terrain; nothing floats or sinks.
- Around the building the blend fades smoothly to the lot edge; outside the lot nothing changed.
- Path ends stay connected to the road or to neighbouring paths.

The suite has checked connectivity and that items stay in the lot; the review judges smoothness and look.
