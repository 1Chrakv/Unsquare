## Checklist: tool window and buttons

- The window shows where the requirement says (above the edited roof, at the screen border, or hidden).
- Labels and icons are readable, not clipped, not overlapping each other or the game's own panels.
- Highlighted, greyed and active states match the session values in `probe.json` (pick kind, gizmo mode,
  undo/redo availability, edit mode, align or level mode).
- The mode button sits where the button position option says, once, highlighted while the mode is on.

The suite has checked the binding values; the review judges what the player actually sees.
