## Checklist: buildings shaped by another mod

- After the conversion the building looks as it did before (same outline, lean and bend within a few
  decimetres), or visibly straighter where the requirement says so.
- No part of the old shape is left behind (doubled walls, a second building, floating props).
- Neighbouring buildings of the same row still meet without gaps or overlaps.

Compare the before and after shots taken from the same camera.
