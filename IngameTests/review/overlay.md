## Checklist: cage overlay

- The cage lines match the building's current shape (or the requested shape while it bakes).
- Colours and styles as the requirement says (e.g. dashed box, hovered or selected highlight, gizmo colours).
- The selected handle and its gizmo sit on the right part of the cage; arrows, square or rings point along the
  building's axes.
- Nothing is drawn that shouldn't be (a ghost cage, a gizmo without a selected handle, a stale cage elsewhere).

Line thickness and exact shades may differ with distance and lighting; judge shape, placement and colour family.
